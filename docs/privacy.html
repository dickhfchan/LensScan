NativeScan Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

NativeScan (“the App”) is provided by Viberobotic LLC. This policy explains how the App handles information.

Summary

NativeScan is designed to work entirely on your device. We do not create accounts, do not run ads or analytics in the App, and do not upload your scans to our servers.

Information the App uses

  • Camera: Used only when you start a scan, to capture document pages.
  • Photo Library (add only): Used only when you choose to save a scanned page to Photos. The App does not read your existing photo library.
  • On-device text recognition: OCR runs locally using Apple frameworks. Recognized text stays on your device unless you share or copy it.

Data storage

Scanned images and related text are stored locally in the App’s private storage on your device. Deleting a scan removes it from that storage. Uninstalling the App removes its local data.

Sharing

If you use Share, Copy, Save to Photos, or export PDF/text, the content you choose leaves the App through the iOS share sheet or Photos. We do not receive that content.

No tracking or third-party analytics

The App does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and does not use App Tracking Transparency identifiers.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
